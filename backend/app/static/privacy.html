Privacy Policy

Retailza Kirana SaaS Store Management Application

Retailza is a proprietary product of TechMasters Innovations Private Limited. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using the Retailza mobile application and associated web platforms.

Information We Collect

Retailza may collect information required to provide our store-management service, including:

  • Mobile number used for login and OTP verification
  • Store owner name and shopkeeper details
  • Shop name, category, and physical address
  • Product and live inventory information (names, purchase price, selling price, barcode, unit measurements, stock levels)
  • Sales, billing, and order transaction records
  • Customer and credit/Khata information entered by the shopkeeper
  • Retailza SaaS subscription and payment verification records
  • Basic technical diagnostic information required for security, uptime, and service operation

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Create and manage your Retailza merchant account
  • Provide shop-management, inventory tracking, and billing features
  • Manage product stock levels and calculate gross margins
  • Record sales and generate digital invoices
  • Manage customer credit/Khata ledgers and repayment logs
  • Display your shop dashboard, daily revenue, and historical metrics
  • Process and verify Retailza Pro SaaS subscriptions
  • Provide prompt customer assistance and support
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse or fraudulent access
  • Improve reliability, cloud availability, and troubleshoot technical errors

Customer Information

Retailza allows shopkeepers to enter customer information such as name, mobile number, address, and credit transactions.

This information is entered directly by the shopkeeper for their own business purposes. Shopkeepers are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate permission or lawful basis to maintain such records.

Payment Information

Retailza subscription payments (for Retailza Pro platform access) are completely separate from customer payments made to the shopkeeper at the store counter.

Retailza uses trusted, RBI-authorized third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay) to process subscription payments. Counter UPI payments transfer directly into the merchant's designated bank account via dynamic NPCI QR codes.

Retailza does not require or store sensitive payment credentials such as UPI PIN, Card PIN, or CVV.

Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption, JWT authentication, hashed credentials, and role-based access controls.

However, no electronic storage or internet-transmitted service can guarantee 100% absolute security.

Data Sharing

We may use trusted service providers required to operate Retailza, such as payment gateways, telecom SMS/OTP providers (e.g. Fast2SMS), cloud hosting, and infrastructure partners.

We do NOT sell or monetize your shop's business records or customer information as a product.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Retailza, maintain statutory business records, provide technical support, preserve security, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable Indian laws.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Retailza account through the application settings or by contacting support. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Account Security

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, or password with anyone. Retailza and TechMasters Innovations Private Limited representatives will never ask you for confidential credentials.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Retailza's services, features, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be made available through the application and on this website.
